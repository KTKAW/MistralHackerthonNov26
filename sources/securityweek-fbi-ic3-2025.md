# SecurityWeek on the FBI's IC3 report for 2025

**In one line:** Losses reported to the FBI reached $20.9bn in 2025.

## What it says

- Reported losses were $20.9bn in 2025, up 26%, across 1,008,597 complaints.
- Investment fraud accounted for $8.6bn.
- Business email compromise (BEC) accounted for about $3bn.
