# UK Finance Annual Fraud Report 2026: press release

**In one line:** UK payment fraud rose again in 2025, and scams that start with a phone call take a share of losses well above their share of cases.

## What it says

- £1.28bn was lost to payment fraud in 2025, up 4%.
- Authorised push payment (APP) fraud was £576.4m, up 19%.
- 66% of APP cases start online. 17% start by phone, and those carry 28% of losses.
- UK businesses lost £75.6m to APP fraud.
