# Microsoft Digital Defense Report 2026, as reported by Help Net Security

**In one line:** Known bugs are turned into working exploits in under a day, while organisations take a month or two to patch.

## What it says

- The median time from discovery of a flaw to a working exploit is "well below 24 hours". Enterprises take 30–60 days to patch critical external flaws.
- Phishing was the way in for 23% of the intrusions Microsoft's incident responders investigated, up from 7% a year earlier.
- Microsoft names identity as the primary control plane.
- Frontier models took over an emulated enterprise domain in a 32-step chain, with no defenders present.
- It cites Sysdig's July 2026 record of the first automated ransomware extortion.
- Most campaigns Microsoft sees are still manually driven.
- On AI bug-finding, it says attackers hold the near-term advantage.

## Caveats

- Not read for this knowledge base. These points come from the [research report of 21 November 2026](research-report-2026-11.md), whose author read the page in full. Check the original before relying on a figure.
- Covers July 2025 to June 2026.
- The research report links the exploit-speed and patch-time figures to [Microsoft's own report page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/security-insider/threat-landscape/2026-digital-defense-report), which it did not read in full.
