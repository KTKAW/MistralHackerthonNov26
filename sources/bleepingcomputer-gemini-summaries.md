# BleepingComputer on hidden text that hijacks Gemini email summaries

**In one line:** Text a person cannot see can steer the AI that summarises an email for them.

## What it says

- Hidden text in an email can steer an AI reader. It was shown against Gmail's Gemini summaries, which then carried the phishing message to the reader.
