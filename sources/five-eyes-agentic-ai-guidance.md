# Careful Adoption of Agentic AI Services: Five Eyes joint guidance

**In one line:** The five national cyber agencies tell organisations to adopt agentic AI slowly and to value resilience over productivity.

## What it says

- Published jointly on 30 April 2026.
- Adopt slowly, start with low-risk tasks, and prioritise resilience over productivity.
- According to [Forrester's write-up](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/five-eyes-cybersecurity-agencies-careful-agentic-ai-adoption-guidance-operationalized-by-aegis/), the guidance says methods for evaluating the security of agentic systems are still evolving.
