# Cyber Security Breaches Survey 2025/26

**In one line:** Nearly half of UK businesses report a breach or attack in a year, most incidents cost little, and basic controls are still missing.

## What it says

- 43% of UK businesses identified a breach or attack. Phishing featured in 38%.
- Only 47% use two-factor authentication.
- The median cost of the most disruptive incident was £0. Most incidents are cheap, and the damage sits in the tail.
- Only 5% of UK businesses hold Cyber Essentials, and 12% of small ones.

- Not read for this knowledge base. These points come from the [research report of 21 November 2026](research-report-2026-11.md), whose author read the page in full. Check the original before relying on a figure.
