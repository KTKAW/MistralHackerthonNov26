# Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026

**In one line:** AI lets one operator work at the scale of a team, and AI keys themselves have become loot.

## What it says

- A majority of the operations Anthropic disrupted were enabled by AI executing or orchestrating them.
- One hacktivist compromised 14 of 42 tracked targets alone.
- One group pulled more than 2,100 Azure AD token sets from more than 40 tenants in about 34 hours.
- Another group used a victim's stolen AI keys to attack about 30 AI companies.
- Fake "cheap Claude" resellers harvested logins.
- Attackers ran TruffleHog over 1.8m Android apps to find hardcoded secrets. Anthropic's advice is to hunt for your own exposed keys and rotate them first.
- Device-code phishing was used against cloud email.
