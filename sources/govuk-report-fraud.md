# GOV.UK: Report Fraud, the new service from City of London Police

**In one line:** Report Fraud is where fraud and cyber crime are reported in the UK.

## What it says

- Report Fraud replaced Action Fraud in December 2025.
- It is reached at [reportfraud.police.uk](https://www.reportfraud.police.uk/) or on 0300 123 2040.
