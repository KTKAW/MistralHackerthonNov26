# Google on Android's scam protection

**In one line:** Android can flag scam calls and texts on the device itself.

## What it says

- Android Scam Detection flags scam speech patterns in calls, and scam texts from non-contacts. Call detection is available in the UK and is off by default.
- Google says it flags more than 5m suspected scam calls and texts a month, according to [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/android-gets-ai-defenses-against-messaging-scams-fake-calls/).
