# Project Glasswing: an initial update

**In one line:** Models now find serious bugs faster than maintainers can fix them, so the bottleneck has moved to triage and patching.

## What it says

- About 50 partners found more than 10,000 high or critical bugs in a month with Mythos Preview.
- High and critical bugs in open source take about two weeks to patch.
- Finding bugs is no longer the bottleneck. Triage and patching are.
- Its advice to defenders: patch faster, and lean on controls that do not depend on any single patch, such as MFA and logging.

## Caveats

- Not read for this knowledge base. These points come from the [research report of 21 November 2026](research-report-2026-11.md), whose author read the page in full. Check the original before relying on a figure.
- Reported by the model maker about its own model.
- The severities are Mythos's own estimates, though 90.6% of the high and critical findings that were triaged held up.
