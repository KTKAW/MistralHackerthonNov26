# Verizon 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report: press release

**In one line:** Exploiting vulnerabilities has overtaken stolen credentials as the top way into organisations.

## What it says

- Vulnerability exploitation was the entry point in 31% of breaches. It is the first time in 19 years that it has beaten stolen credentials.
- 48% of breaches involved a third party, up 60%.
- Social engineering on mobile, by text and voice, succeeds 40% more often than email phishing.

## Caveats

- Not read for this knowledge base. These points come from the [research report of 21 November 2026](research-report-2026-11.md), whose author read the page in full. Check the original before relying on a figure.
- Covers 2025 data. The data ends in October 2025, before Mythos-class models.
